# Changelog – draft-attoumani-ietf-inclusion

## Version -05 (11 August 2026)

- Introduced the concept of IETF awareness as a prerequisite for sustainable inclusion (new Section 13).
- Added the IETF Participation Continuum model, describing pathways from awareness to technical contribution and community leadership (new Section 12).
- Added discussion of community partnerships and collaborative approaches involving academia and Internet governance communities.
- Added an illustrative pilot initiative related to the IGF 2026 workshop proposal "Standards for All: Bringing Global South Academia to IETF" (new Section 11).
- Added an appendix "Changes since -04".

## Version -04 (8 February 2026)

- Major expansion of the document from 6 to 11 sections.
- Added "Current Participation Metrics and Gaps" (Section 2).
- Added "Alignment with Global Digital Governance Frameworks" (SDG 9, ITU Connect 2030, AU Digital Transformation Strategy, ISOC Action Plan, UN Roadmap for Digital Cooperation).
- Added "Voices from the Community", a synthesis of regional discussions including Africa IGF 2025.
- Added "Addressing Common Concerns" and "Communicating the Value Proposition".
- Expanded "Proposed Directions": transformative impact of a meeting in an underrepresented region (9.3.1), shared responsibility and multistakeholder advocacy (9.7), proposed IETF Africa Engagement Program pilot (9.8), proposed Global Inclusion Working Group (9.9), proposed success metrics for 2027 and 2030 (9.10).
- Added "Immediate Next Steps" and an appendix with a draft implementation roadmap.
- Single-author document (K. Attoumani Mohamed).
